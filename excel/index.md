# Suncal - Excel Tools

These Excel spreadsheets perform basic calculations of uncertainties and measurement decision risk.
No VBA macros are used, but because they make use of LAMBDA functions, they require Excel 365 or 2024.

- [Measurement Decision Risk Calculator](<PSL Measurement Decision Risk Calculator v0.1.xlsx>)
- [Calibration Interval Calculator (A3, S2, V1)](<PSL Calibration Interval Calculator v0.1.xlsx>)
